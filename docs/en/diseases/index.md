## How to use this section

- **Similar signs, different causes**: respiratory and digestive signs overlap heavily between pathogen groups, so test before you medicate;
- **Differential diagnosis is key**: oral white plaques may be trichomonads, Candida or the mucosal form of pigeon pox, and the treatment for each is completely different;
- **Watch for zoonoses**: Salmonella and Chlamydia psittaci can infect people, so always wash and disinfect your hands after handling sick pigeons.

> **Scientific statement**: signs alone cannot confirm a diagnosis; laboratory testing (qPCR and similar) is the basis for confirmation. The content on this site is an educational reference and does not constitute veterinary diagnostic advice.
