# Parasite Testing in Racing Pigeons

Parasites are a common pathogen category in racing pigeons. This page collects testing and control knowledge for the most important protozoal pathogen of the species — Trichomonas gallinae.

## Pathogen Overview

| Pathogen | English | Test method |
|---|---|---|
| [Pigeon trichomonads](../diseases/ge-maodichong.md) | Trichomonas gallinae | Real-time fluorescent PCR / microscopy |

## Characteristics of Trichomonads

- **Spread orally**: feeding of squabs by the breeding pair and shared drinkers are the main transmission routes;
- **Typical lesion**: yellow-white caseous pseudomembranes in the oral cavity and throat;
- **Drinking-water management is key**: contaminated drinkers are an important vehicle for spread within the flock;
- **Differential diagnosis matters**: oral white plaques must be distinguished from candidiasis and the mucosal form of pigeon pox, and the medication used is completely different.

## Control Principles

- Clean drinkers daily and disinfect them on a regular schedule;
- Check breeding stock before a race to reduce transmission through feeding;
- Follow a proper deworming protocol and avoid indiscriminate medication;
- Isolate affected pigeons and control stocking density.
