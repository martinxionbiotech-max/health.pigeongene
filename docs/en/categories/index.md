# Pathogen Categories in Racing Pigeons

Racing pigeon pathogens fall into three broad categories: viruses, bacteria and parasites (protozoa). This page is the category index.

## Pathogen Categories

- [Virus testing](viruses.md) — transmission, signs, diagnosis and control for 7 common racing pigeon viruses;
- [Bacterial testing](bacteria.md) — transmission, signs, diagnosis and control for Salmonella spp., Chlamydia psittaci and Mycoplasma spp.;
- [Parasite testing](parasites.md) — transmission, signs, diagnosis and control for Trichomonas gallinae.

## Why the Category Matters

- **Different drugs**: antivirals, antibiotics and antiparasitic drugs each target entirely different organisms;
- **Different control strategies**: for viruses the emphasis is immunity and biosecurity, for bacteria hygiene and disciplined medication use, for parasites drinking-water management and deworming;
- **Different testing methods**: each pathogen group is matched to a different test (PCR / culture / microscopy).

## Testing Tips

- When signs overlap, a combined multi-pathogen panel is advisable;
- Treat only after confirmation, to avoid blind medication and resistance;
- Quarantine of newly introduced birds is the first line of defence against bringing a new pathogen in.
