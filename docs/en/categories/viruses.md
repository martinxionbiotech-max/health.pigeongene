# Virus Testing in Racing Pigeons

Viruses are a major pathogen group in racing pigeon disease. This page collects testing and control knowledge for 7 common pigeon viruses; every pathogen page covers transmission, signs, differential diagnosis and prevention points.

| [Newcastle disease virus](../diseases/xinchengyi-bingdu.md) | Newcastle Disease Virus (NDV) | Real-time RT-PCR |

## Common Features of Viral Disease

- **Overlapping signs**: respiratory, digestive and neurological signs look very similar across several viral diseases, so signs alone cannot confirm a diagnosis;
- **Set off by stress**: race marking, transport and moulting are the stress events that often trigger viral disease;
- **Immune-related**: immunosuppression caused by circovirus and similar agents opens the door to other pathogens;
- **Confirm by testing**: real-time PCR/RT-PCR is the main route to confirmation.

## Control Principles

- Vaccination (Newcastle disease and others) and antibody monitoring;
- Biosecurity: isolation, disinfection, density control, less stress;
- Quarantine on introduction and regular monitoring, to block transmission by silent carriers.
