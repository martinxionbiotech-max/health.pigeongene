# Bacterial Testing in Racing Pigeons

Bacteria are a major pathogen category in racing pigeon disease. This page collects testing and control knowledge for 3 groups of common bacterial pathogens in racing pigeons.

## Pathogen Overview

| Pathogen | English | Test method |
|---|---|---|
| [Salmonella](../diseases/shamen-shijun.md) | Salmonella spp. | Real-time PCR / bacterial culture |
| [Chlamydia psittaci](../diseases/yingwure-yiyuanti.md) | Chlamydia psittaci | Real-time PCR |
| [Mycoplasma](../diseases/zhiyuanti.md) | Mycoplasma spp. | Real-time PCR |

## Common Features of Bacterial Disease

- **Zoonotic risk**: both Salmonella spp. and Chlamydia psittaci can infect people, so always wash and disinfect your hands after handling sick birds;
- **Tendency to become chronic**: Mycoplasma spp. and similar agents readily turn into chronic infection with repeated flare-ups;
- **Let susceptibility testing guide treatment**: irregular antibiotic use drives resistance, so treat on the basis of pathogen testing and susceptibility results;
- **Environmental spread**: contaminated feed, drinking water and dust are the main vehicles.

## Control Principles

- Rodent control and wild-bird exclusion, and clean feed and drinking water;
- Damp cleaning to cut dust (Chlamydia spreads on dust);
- Quarantine newly introduced birds, remove persistently shedding individuals;
- Disciplined medication: full course, guided by susceptibility testing, no unnecessary use.
