# Racing Pigeon Health Library

The PigeonGene Health Library (health.senopigeon.com) is the health backbone of the PigeonGene digital ecosystem. It provides **scientific and carefully worded** racing pigeon disease and testing knowledge for fanciers.

## What you will find here

- [Pathogen encyclopedia](diseases/index.md): 12 common racing pigeon pathogens (7 viruses, 3 bacteria, 1 fungus, 1 parasite). Each page covers transmission, common signs, differential diagnosis, testing methods and control points.
- [Testing and prevention guides](guides/index.md): eight practical guides covering PCR testing, biosecurity, symptom recognition, disease prevention, loft health and diagnostic methods.
- [Pathogen categories](categories/index.md): what virus, bacterium and parasite classification means for testing and treatment.
- [Glossary](glossary.md) and [FAQ](faq.md).

## Who this is for

- **Fanciers**: recognise suspicious signs and know when laboratory testing is warranted;
- **Loft managers**: apply isolation, disinfection and quarantine-on-introduction practice;
- **Veterinarians and researchers**: look up pathogen characteristics and testing methods quickly.

## Scientific statement

> **Signs are not the same thing as a diagnosis.** When suspicious signs appear, confirm the pathogen through laboratory testing (real-time PCR) rather than treating on signs alone. The content here is educational reference only and does not constitute veterinary advice.
